This is a *recomputed*, hand-typed chart of frames 022 and 023, pages B-8                                                       
and B-9 of the microfilmed copy of the Soil Mechanics Experiment Report of                                                      
Apollo 16 LSRP Data, May 1972.  The corresponding JPG images of these frames                                                    
in this dataset are /DOCUMENT/A16_SMSS_72_M016936_022.JPG and                                                                   
/DOCUMENT/A16_SMSS_72_M016936_023.JPG.                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                
Chart checked with original scans January 17, 2011                                                                              
                                                                                                                                
Notes:                                                                                                                          
- In this chart the conversion from drum load to drum circumference                                                             
  deflection was recomputed by the data producer for all rows using the                                                         
  multiplier given on the original chart, 0.5542.  On the original scanned                                                      
  chart, the conversion from Drum Load to Drum Circumference Deflection is                                                      
  given as 'x .5542', and the conversion of Delta Drum Depth reading to                                                         
  Actual Penetration Depth is 'x 31.3'.  However it appears the multipliers                                                     
  used to derive drum circumference deflection from drum load were 0.542 for                                                    
  the first 24 rows and approximately 0.553 for the remaining rows listed on                                                    
  the chart.  The original scanned chart is provided as a separate table                                                        
  A16_SMSS_72_M016936_022_ORI.TAB, along with a header file and PDS label,                                                      
  A16_SMSS_72_M016936_022_ORI.TXT and A16_SMSS_72_M016936_022_ORI.LBL.                                                          
- On the scanned chart, Load column heading has a notation below it:                                                            
  '(from calib. curve)'                                                                                                         
- On the scanned chart, the Initial Drum Depth/Final column does not have any                                                   
  final values recorded; only initial values are given.                                                                         
- At the bottom of each scanned chart is 'Treadwell & Moriwaki'                                                                 
- Superscript '2', an exponential, is denoted by '**2' in the table below.                                                      
- The exact numbers on the charts have been reproduced even when they appear                                                    
  to be in error based on the expected multipliers, conversions, etc.  Where                                                    
  obvious, the apparent discrepancies are noted in the last column.  The order                                                  
  the numbers were calculated in is not known, so it is difficult to determine                                                  
  how mistakes effect subsequent calculations, therefore we have not attempted                                                  
  to redo the calculations.
