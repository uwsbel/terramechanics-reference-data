This is a hand-typed chart of frame 032, page B-18 of the microfilmed copy of                                                   
the Soil Mechanics Experiment Report of Apollo 16 LSRP Data, May 1972.  The                                                     
corresponding JPG image of this frame in this dataset is                                                                        
/DOCUMENT/A16_SMSS_72_M016936_032.JPG.                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                
Chart checked with original scans January 17, 2011                                                                              
                                                                                                                                
Notes:                                                                                                                          
- On the scanned chart, the conversion from Drum Load to Drum Circumference                                                     
  Deflection is given as 'x .5542', and the conversion of Delta Drum Depth                                                      
  reading to Actual Penetration Depth is 'x 31.3'.                                                                              
- On the scanned chart, Load column heading has a notation below it:                                                            
  '(from calib. curve)'                                                                                                         
- On the scanned chart, the Initial Drum Depth/Final column does not have any                                                   
  final values recorded; only initial values are given.                                                                         
- At the bottom of each scanned chart is 'Treadwell & Moriwaki'                                                                 
- Superscript '2', an exponential, is denoted by '**2' in the table below.                                                      
- The exact numbers on the charts have been reproduced even when they appear                                                    
  to be in error based on the expected multipliers, conversions, etc.  Where                                                    
  obvious, the apparent discrepancies are noted in the last column.  The order                                                  
  the numbers were calculated in is not known, so it is difficult to determine                                                  
  how mistakes effect subsequent calculations, therefore we have not attempted                                                  
  to redo the calculations.
