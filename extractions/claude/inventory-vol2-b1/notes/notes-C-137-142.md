General note on C-8 to C-15: the printed cohesion values are intercepts of least-squares lines (apparent cohesion), the quantity Vol. I says not to use as a descriptor; each page states the average density of the series. These are derived-series rows, each from a group of tests at one nominal state; where a matching data sheet exists in C-1 to C-7 the relation is derived-from, otherwise the underlying tests are unpublished.
