- 105 (printed 2): text: piezoelectric stress gages at each end (forward gage cemented outside the sample, Fig. 2; reaction gage inside the end plate face); loose samples poured through a slotted funnel; dense samples vibrated with a small plate; confining pressure by partial vacuum inside a rubber diaphragm; several confining pressures per sample; axial stress from the shock tube driver pressurized to the same pressure each time; peak stress applied under 2 psi. Results: E = rho c^2 (equation 1); plotted against sigma_3 in Figs 3 to 7; modulus decreases with lower confining pressure or density; LSM results questionable because of a confining pressure gradient along the specimen (low permeability). Setup evidence, insight.
- 106 (printed 3): text: LSM gradient; Discussion: equation (2) E = rho c^2 (1+nu)(1-2nu)/(1-nu); f(nu) plotted in Fig. 8; for nu under 0.20 the error of neglecting it is under 10 percent; recommends measuring nu. Insight (the Table 2-3 moduli are constrained moduli, not Young's moduli, unless nu is small).
