## Appendix A summary

Per soil: a specification table (chemical analysis), a photomicrograph, a grain size figure (average and range of 3 to 7 samples with an inset table of D50, D10, Cu, D95), a density versus relative density figure with 95 percent confidence limits (printed endpoints), and a phi versus Dr figure. Soils sharing a material (1, 3, 4 RS; 7, 8, 9 SS; 10, 11 LSM) repeat the same four figures under new numbers. Distinct figure content: RS (A-1 to A-4), PS (A-5 to A-8), RSM-a (A-17 to A-20), RC2 (A-21 to A-24), SS (A-25 to A-28), LSM (A-37 to A-40), RSM-b (A-45 to A-48): 28 distinct figures of 48 numbered. Three sonic moduli in Table 2-3 are estimates, not measurements (soils 5, 10, 12). Placement procedures per soil are stated (hopper; roller passes). Degradation comments: RS, PS, RSM-a, RSM-b susceptible; SS not; RC2 and LSM shoveled.
