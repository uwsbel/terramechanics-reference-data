# Setup

Reader: Claude (blind). Source: AM-68-1 Vol. I (NASA CR-92208). Units below are as printed; nothing here is
converted. Statements marked "computed" or "inferred" are this reader's, not the report's.

## Footpad impact runs (b68-imp-4, -10, -12, -15, -16, -ii-1, -fs16)

Where each setup fact is printed:

1. Footpad diameter 6.17 inches for Runs No. 4, 10, 12 (legend inset "Footpad Diameter = 6.17 Inches"):
   AM-68-1 Vol. I, Fig. 4-19, printed 4-23, PDF 208; repeated under the caption of Fig. 4-20, printed 4-24,
   PDF 209; and for Runs No. 4, 15, 16 in the inset of Fig. 4-21, printed 4-25, PDF 210. Fig. 4-22, printed
   4-26, PDF 211, gives "6.17-and 37-Inch Diameter Footpads" in the caption only; the pairing of 6.17 inch with
   the subscale panel and 37 inch with the full-scale panel follows from the panel labels.
2. Relative density per run: Dr = 0, 0.45, 0.65 for Runs No. 4, 10, 12 (Fig. 4-19 and Fig. 4-20 legends);
   "Relative Density = 0" for Runs No. 4, 15, 16 (Fig. 4-21 inset). No Dr is printed for Test No. II-1 or
   Full Scale Test No. 16.
3. Soil and state: Fig. 4-19 and 4-20 captions say "Various RS Soils"; Fig. 4-21 caption says "RS Loose Soil";
   the paragraph on AM-68-1 Vol. I, text printed 4-22, PDF 207, says Fig. 4-19 shows "RS loose, intermediate,
   and dense soil" (matched here to the legend order Dr 0, 0.45, 0.65) and that Fig. 4-22 is "on RS loose
   soil". No unit weight and no dynamic penetrometer gp is printed for any of these runs.
4. Moving weight M1: 31, 41, 51 lbs. for Runs No. 4, 15, 16 (Fig. 4-21 legend; text printed 4-22, PDF 207);
   31 lbs. for Subscale Oblique Impact Test No. II-1 and 1140 lbs. for Full Scale Oblique Impact Test No. 16
   (Fig. 4-22 panel labels). Not printed for Runs No. 10 and 12. The rig ranges are "about 32 pounds" to
   "about 50 pounds" for the subscale bar (text printed 3-22, PDF 163) and "about 570 pounds" to "about 1300
   pounds" for the full-scale ram (text printed 3-32, PDF 173); the impacting mass is footpad plus load cell
   plus bar or ram plus lead weights.
5. Direction and velocity: all are vertical impacts (captions of Figs. 4-19 to 4-22) at "about six fps" (text
   printed 4-22, PDF 207). The nominal velocity grid is 1, 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 fps subscale and 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 fps
   full scale (Fig. 4-17, printed 4-21, PDF 206). No measured velocity is printed for any of these runs.
6. Soil bed: subscale, "the three feet by eight feet by two feet deep soil bed" (text printed 3-17, PDF 158;
   text printed 3-20, PDF 161, says a new and larger soil bin was built for the modified rig); full scale,
   "the 12-by 18-foot by 5-foot deep soil pit" (text printed 3-27, PDF 168). The full-scale pit and the model
   drop bin were filled with the same large hopper (text printed 2-107, PDF 132). Neither bed depth nor bed
   preparation is stated per run.
7. Footpads: subscale, a 1/6-scale LM footpad instrumented for pitch and roll (text printed 3-19, PDF 160;
   drawing Fig. 3-15, printed 3-24, PDF 165); full scale, identical to the LM footpad in size and contour,
   epoxy/glass fiber with a spun-aluminum skin (text printed 3-35, PDF 176; drawing Fig. 3-26, printed 3-36,
   PDF 177, dimensions barely legible in the scan).
8. Rig behavior: both rigs are air guns; the bar or ram is accelerated by air pressure and gravity and then
   travels as a free body except for slight bearing friction (text printed 3-20, PDF 161; text printed 3-32,
   PDF 173). Displacement comes from a slide wire potentiometer on the bar or ram, and the report says the
   displacement record was the only measure of footpad velocity during impact (text printed 4-7, PDF 192).
9. Gravity: not stated per run. Text printed 4-79, PDF 264, separates "tests at earth gravity" from the
   reduced-gravity tests of Appendix E, which implies (inferred) that these runs were at earth gravity.

What a simulator user still has to assume:

1. The impact velocity of each run (only "about six fps" is printed). Fig. 4-40 (printed 4-53, PDF 238) lists
   initial velocities of analog-matched tests (for example RS Loose 1/6 scale 52.7 to 179 in./sec.) but does
   not name the runs, so it cannot be tied to these rows.
2. The bed unit weight (only Dr is printed, and Dr 0.65 for Run No. 12 does not match soil 4 at Dr 0.80), the
   bed preparation method and the fill depth.
3. The moving mass of Runs No. 10 and 12.
4. The subscale footpad contour (diameter only is printed beside the data) and the full-scale contour.
5. Earth gravity, the time zero and displacement zero of the records, and any filtering of the oscillograph
   traces.
6. Whether the bed was fresh for each run (not stated; mcc_relevant set to no by default).

## 1/6-scale LM model drops (b68-mdrop-10 = NASA Test No. 1, b68-mdrop-11 = NASA Test No. 2)

Where each setup fact is printed:

1. Drop identity and conditions: Table 5-T2, printed 5-28, PDF 308, rows 10 (7-18-67) and 11 (7-19-67): test
   type C (detail correlation; key on printed 5-32, PDF 312), RS Loose, VV 10 and 5, VH 0 and 7 (no unit
   printed in the table), ground slope 0, yaw 45 deg, Dr 0, density 46.9 Lbs./Ft.3, friction angle 41 deg,
   stable Yes, remarks "NASA Drop No. 1. Tests No. 1-8 McDonnell reduced." and "NASA Drop No. 2."
2. Touchdown velocities: Table 5-T3, printed 5-45, PDF 325: NASA Test No. 1 = Bendix drop 10, VV -114.9 and Vh
   0 In./Sec., tau .298 sec; NASA Test No. 2 = Bendix drop 11, VV -60.5 and Vh 83.5 In./Sec., tau .177 sec;
   both RS Loose, Dr 0, 46.9 Lb./Ft.3, phi 41 deg. The strut-load figure insets print "Vv = -114.93 in. sec.
   Vh = 0, RS Loose, Level Surface" (Fig. 5-25, printed 5-50, PDF 330) and "Vv = -60.55 In. Sec., Vh = 83.75
   In. Sec., RS Loose, Level Surface" (Fig. 5-32, printed 5-57, PDF 337; degraded scan, leading digits
   uncertain). Text printed 5-44, PDF 324, gives the nominal conditions (pure vertical drop at "teen" (ten) feet
   per second; five feet per second vertical with seven horizontal) and says the table velocities were
   computed from the release-to-contact time measured with the x accelerometer.
3. Attitude: zero angular rates, zero pitch and roll, 45-degree yaw (2-2 landing), level landing surface (text
   printed 5-44, PDF 324; text printed 5-43, PDF 323).
4. Model mass properties: total model weight 68.4 Lbs., inertias Io(Xb) 1.6022, Io(Yb) 1.2880, Io(Zb) 1.3402,
   Io(Ys) 0.09917, Io(Zs) 0.09914 slug ft.2, initial CG height 23.90 inches, primary strut assembly weight 1.82
   Lbs. with its CG 14.66 inches from the upper hinge, footpad to body centerline radius 27.928 inches
   (unnumbered table, printed 5-22, PDF 302); geometry and 68.4 Lbs. also in Fig. 5-9, printed 5-15, PDF 295.
   Scale factors in Table 5-T1, printed 5-2, PDF 281.
5. Landing gear: crushable aluminum honeycomb capsules in telescopic struts (text printed 5-2, PDF 281); load
   versus stroke of model and LM struts in Fig. 5-10, printed 5-16, PDF 296; capsule crush loads within plus or
   minus 10 percent, primary strut compression 151 to 183 pounds, measured dynamic loads usually 200 to 225
   pounds (text printed 5-17 and 5-18, PDF 297 and 298).
6. Footpads: "Scaled LM footpads were added to each leg" (text printed 5-4, PDF 283); ball joints with stops at
   24 degrees, pads set against the stop and tilted 2 degrees in the touchdown position, footpad No. 1 on a
   non-yawing universal joint (text printed 5-9, PDF 289; Fig. 5-6, printed 5-11, PDF 291). No footpad
   diameter is printed in Section V.
7. Soil bed: 18 feet long, 12 feet wide, maximum below-ground depth five feet, depth adjusted to the test (text
   printed 5-22, PDF 302); loose RS and SS beds placed by the spreader in about four-inch layers with no
   further handling (text printed 5-43, PDF 323); dynamic penetrometer readings after each drop and the soil
   completely removed before a new bed (text printed 5-44, PDF 324), so each drop is on a fresh bed.
8. Data reduction: 13 tape signals filtered and digitized every 1/1000 second and reduced by McDonnell (text
   printed 5-43, PDF 323); the plotted parameters are listed on text printed 5-46, PDF 326.

What a simulator user still has to assume:

1. The footpad diameter and contour (only "scaled LM footpads" is printed; 37/6 = 6.17 inch would be an
   inference from the scale factor, not a printed value).
2. The depth of the loose RS bed (18 inches is printed only for the RS intermediate beds).
3. The bed state: the printed 46.9 pcf at Dr 0 does not match soil 1 (RS Loose, 41.4 pcf at Dr 0, Table 2-3),
   so either the batch, the density reference or the Dr calibration differs; a user must choose which number
   to honor.
4. Gravity: not stated. Table 5-T1 (velocity ratio 1.0, acceleration ratio 1/6) is consistent with a 1/6-scale
   model dropped at earth gravity to represent a full-scale lunar landing (inferred).
5. Initial conditions at touchdown should come from Table 5-T3, not from tau: computed, 386.09 in./sec.2 times
   .298 sec gives 115.0 in./sec. (agrees with -114.9 for Test No. 1), but times .177 sec gives 68.3 in./sec.
   against the printed -60.5 for Test No. 2, so the Test No. 2 vertical velocity is not g times tau and the
   report does not say how the release with horizontal velocity was reduced.
6. Strut crush curves beyond the nominal Fig. 5-10 shapes, joint friction, footpad-soil friction, and the time
   origin of the plotted histories.
