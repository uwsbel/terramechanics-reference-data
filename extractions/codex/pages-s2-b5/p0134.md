## 2.8.2 Preparation of Intermediate Soils.

Several methods of producing intermediate densities (i.e. $0.3 ≤ D_r ≤ 0.7$) in the test soils have been investigated. These include deposition by free-fall from a fixed height, screeding, and mechanical compaction utilizing both static and vibratory methods.

### 2.8.2.1 Free-Fall Method.

A range of loose through intermediate densities can be obtained in a granular soil bed by varying the distance through which the soil falls in being deposited. Loose soils are obtained by keeping the distance of free-fall to a minimum, and successively higher densities are produced by increasing the distance of free-fall. This method was used in the small Load-Penetration Test (LPT) bin and, when the height of drop was carefully controlled, fairly uniform intermediate densities were obtained.
