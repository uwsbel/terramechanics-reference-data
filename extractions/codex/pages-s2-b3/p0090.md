Test Bin 108 Inches by 36 Inches

Slope = $AγN_q$ = 54 lbs./in.

$$∴ N_q = \frac{54}{Aγ} = \frac{54(1730)}{12.6(108)} ≅ 69$$

∴ φ ≅ 39°

φ (Direct Shear Test) = 39.5°

Intercept = 140 lbs. = $0.3 ADγN_γ$

$$N_γ = \frac{140(1730)}{0.3(12.6)\ 4(108)} = 148$$

⇒φ = 42°
