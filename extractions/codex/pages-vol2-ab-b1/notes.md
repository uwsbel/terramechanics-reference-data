## Protocol applications where the layout needed interpretation

- The inline numbered specification fields 1–4 are retained as numbered paragraphs. Standalone numbered titles 5–14 are Markdown headings and their numbers are listed in `sections`. The appendix title supplies section A on PDF 2.
- The supplied chemical and mineral table locator IDs are in the headers. These tables have no printed `Table ...` caption; none was invented. Grain-size inset tables have no supplied separate table IDs, so their pages retain `tables: []` while their cells are fully transcribed in the body.
- Markdown cannot merge cells. The inset tables use two heading rows to retain `Value`, `Average`, and `Range` once each. The soil classification strips likewise retain merged-cell text once, leaving continuation cells blank.
- The grain-size tables are body Markdown tables adjacent to the figure block. The figure blocks retain only caption, axes, legend, and one-sentence description. The general grain-size title, classification strip, and photograph scale notes are body text.
- Empty `axes:` and `legend:` fields mean none are printed. No inferred line-series names were supplied. The visible PS label in the photograph is retained as a legend entry.
- Axis tick labels and sieve-number ticks are not transcribed, following rule 9's exclusion of tick ranges. Explicit numerical callouts outside the density plot frames are body text, as required for outside-frame annotations; they are not curve readings.
- PDF 12's direct-shear prose resumes on PDF 17 after four figure pages. Continuation markers link those two prose pieces; no body text was invented on the intervening figure pages.
- No display equations occur on these pages. Chemical and grain-size subscripts use single-dollar math with Unicode text.
