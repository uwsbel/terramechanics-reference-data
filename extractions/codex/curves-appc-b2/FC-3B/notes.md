# FC-3B independent reading

Read only packet/p0124.png, packet metadata, and CURVE-READING.md. Coordinates refer to the supplied unrotated 3213 by 4508 image. No external or other-reader readings were consulted.

Calibration: detect was run separately for each panel. Final upper settings: box (680,1265,2148,2745), 24 bands, min-frac .55, snap 4, frame-frac .6. Final lower settings: (680,2865,2148,3462), 20 bands, otherwise same. Initial detection was rejected because a 3000-psf track followed nearby curve ink, and a lower left-frame track followed marker ink. The final fits were checked against frame and grid strokes. No final line is predicted. The lower panel has only +50, 0 and -150 horizontal lines, unequally spaced: the fit uses the two frame endpoints, with zero checked independently. Do not supply these three labels to the equal-spacing detector as if the lines were equally spaced.

Eye checks are in calibration-eye-checks.csv. The x checks use v=2700 (upper) and v=3400 (lower), and the y checks use u=700. Upper x labels 0,100,250 and y labels 0,2000,5000; lower x labels 0,100,250 and y labels +50,0,-150. Upper RMS residuals: x .297 thousandths, y 7.936 psf; lower: x .288 thousandths, y .159 thousandths. Slightly uneven printed grid spacing is retained by the affine least-squares fit. Residuals are below .5% of each axis span.

Method: markers were identified before traces from the labeled zoom-3 crops. Their by-eye seed coordinates are preserved in reading-spec.json. For isolated symbols the helper refines the center to the midpoint of the local ink component bounding box; it does not refine components attached to the grid or frame. Clean centers have 2 px bounds. Ambiguous centers have 5-10 px bounds, noted row by row. A triangle center means the midpoint of its drawn bounding box, not the geometric centroid.

The drawn upper curves were visually followed using independent pixel guides. The helper selects centers of actual dark-column runs near those guides, at a nominal 12 px spacing (~2.05 thousandths), with small column adjustments to avoid vertical gridlines. Trace bounds are half the ink-run thickness. No interpolated y value is emitted: guide interpolation only selects a search region. Every emitted trace row is a reading of dark ink, and the overlay was visually checked. Where a horizontal grid shares ink with the circle plateau, bounds are widened. Visible interruptions around markers are not filled by interpolation. The sampling audit lists skipped target columns and maximum gaps; gaps over 5 thousandths occur at symbols/deliberate blank interruptions or masked grid intersections. Additional gaps are not observations. Features identify the first plotted symbol and the peak/last drawn ink; a broad crest does not imply a unique peak displacement. Lower panel has no drawn lines, so contains markers only.

Legend: n4695 plus, n3815 circle, n2934 triangle-down, n1761 square, n734 triangle-up. The legend prints tau instead of sigma in the normal-stress heading. Values and densities follow the printed legend, including 59.4 for n734.

Ambiguities and omissions:
- Lower x=0: triangle-up and square are close and attached to the frame; triangle-down and plus also overlap the frame. Bounds are 10 px. A separate n3815 circle near x=0 and about -95 to -100 thousandths cannot be identified; it may share the plus/frame at (684,3330). It is omitted, not invented. n3815 thus has 12 lower markers while the other series have 13.
- Upper n2934 has 11 visible triangles; the curve ends near u=2117, but there is no distinguishable final triangle at the 250-thousandths frame. This possible missing marker is noted rather than supplied.
- Upper n734 has no visible marker near u=1550 (~150 thousandths); the line continues through that region. It has 11 visible triangles. Several triangles, especially around 110 and 130 thousandths, sit above the drawn curve; trace follows line ink, not marker interpolation.
- n3815 upper late circles and drawn plateau almost coincide with the 3000-psf grid. The final circle overlaps the right frame. Plateau stroke may be doubled; trace bounds include the combined ink thickness.
- Other grid/frame contacts are annotated in the CSVs. Lower squares near u=1149 and u=1842 have broken or grid-obscured sides. Frame marker centers can map just outside nominal x range; coordinates were not clamped.
- The upper first curves start just after the first symbols; first feature rows explicitly denote first plotted symbols. Last feature rows denote final visible curve segments before terminal symbols.

Files: cal-p1/ and cal-p2/ each contain anchors.csv and calibration.json; calibration-eye-checks.csv; points-p1.csv and points-p1-native.csv; points-p2.csv and points-p2-native.csv; overlay-p1.png and overlay-p2.png. Supporting reading-spec.json, counts.json, trace-sampling-audit.json, labeled reading crops/tiles and frame-p2.png preserve the reading process. The common helper is out/read_helpers.py.

Final bounds review: peak feature horizontal bounds were widened to cover the visually plausible crest/plateau location. Vertical bounds remain tied to ink/center reading uncertainty. The final point tables were regenerated with the common helper after its symbol-mask review; no interpolated point values were introduced.

Marker-center audit: final refinement also uses closed white interiors of hollow symbols and the two orthogonal bars of pluses when the outline joins line ink. Triangle interior-box centers receive a 1.5-px correction toward the vertex to recover outer symbol-box centers, with 5-px bounds when the outer outline is connected to other ink. If neither local method nor an isolated ink box succeeds, the by-eye center is retained with at least 6-px bounds and a row note. marker-center-audit.json records every seed, final center, method and bound. Final overlays were reviewed after refinement.

Final trace QA: symbol masks use two-dimensional extents, permitting readings on lines that pass distinctly above/below off-curve markers. All panel symbols are located before masks are applied. Candidate points that follow another guide branch more closely are excluded unless shared ink is explicitly flagged. Horizontal-grid ink is accepted only when the intended branch itself lies within 10 px of that grid. Search radius is 28 px; extra by-eye guide locations resolve curved initial limbs without expanding to unrelated ink. Only emitted ink samples, not guides, appear in points CSVs.

trace-gaps-over-5.csv enumerates each retained over-5-thousandths gap with its neighboring samples and nearby symbols. This documents where the requested regular coverage is interrupted, rather than supplying invented points.

The contact sheet unisolated-centers-review.png documents final targeted checks of attached/broken symbol outlines. Centers were corrected where the initial seed was offset from the visible symbol. Final marker-center-audit.json records the corrected seed positions. Trace masks were regenerated from these positions to prevent sampling symbol ink as a curve.

Final reproducibility: use the final reading-spec.json with out/read_helpers.py to regenerate tables and overlays. Temporary procedural editing scripts were removed; final specs and image crops are retained. Clean disconnected triangle outlines use the outer ink bounding box; connected outlines use the white-interior method with 5-px bounds.
