## Principal ambiguities

- FC-3B: circle plateau shares 3000-psf grid ink; lower x=0 circle not separable; possible missing upper terminal triangle-down and triangle-up near 150 thousandths.
- FC-3C: heading hides most of 6000-psf frame; possible missing upper square near 130 thousandths; crowded lower circle/triangle/plus pairs.
- FC-4B: uneven y grid; several upper markers depart from drawn curves; tiny lower circles and partially shared clusters, including one circle beyond right frame; no identifiable lower plus at x=0/200.
- FC-4C: n2934 is triangle-up and n741 triangle-down; unlabeled outer top frame above 8000; early and late shared curve ink; lower frame/zero clusters, possible hidden triangle-ups, and a small circle beyond left frame.

Trace sampling is nominally about 2.06 thousandths. Gaps exceeding 5 are explicitly retained at symbol interruptions, excluded grid contacts or inseparable ink. No interpolated y values are emitted. The audit JSON lists gaps/columns so this exception to uniform coverage is reviewable.
