Evidence: packet/p0127.png and packet metadata/protocol only. This original page is 3263 by 4508 pixels, unlike the 3213-wide neighboring pages. Coordinates are on this page without resizing or rotation. No other reading was consulted.

Lower box (790,2790,2275,3410), bands 20, min-frac .55, snap 4, frame-frac .6; y endpoints -150,+50. Initial boxes clipped bottom frames, so wrong detected bottom values were rejected and boxes enlarged before reading. All final lines are detected, none predicted. The zero lower horizontal line is an independent check, not an equally spaced intermediate anchor.

Upper RMS residuals: x .299 thousandths, y 20.588 psf. Maximum x .584 thousandths, maximum y 35.687 psf. Lower RMS x .235 thousandths, y .038 thousandths; maxima .472 and .059. Upper RMS is .412% of the y span; maximum is .714%, above the protocol's .5% criterion if interpreted as a maximum-residual criterion. Changing the box corrected the gross detection error, but by-eye inspection confirms that the printed top interval is physically shorter than the other intervals (5000 frame near v=1204, 4000 line near v=1481 at u=900; subsequent intervals about 291-297 px). A single affine fit cannot remove this real grid-spacing discrepancy. The required affine least-squares fit is retained, and this limitation is explicitly flagged; its native bounds add RMS, not maximum model discrepancy. Near the top, fitted 5000 position is ~9 px above the actual frame. Do not interpret the small x RMS as evidence of exact y calibration.

Eye checks in calibration-eye-checks.csv: upper x 0,100,250 at v=2600; upper y 5000,3000,0 at u=900; lower x 0,100,250 at v=3350; lower y +50,0,-150 at u=900. These positions were read against the labeled crops and page. The lower zero agrees independently.

Method: labeled zoom-3 crops, markers identified first. Manual seeds are preserved in reading-spec.json. Clean centers were refined to isolated local ink-component bounding-box centers. Clean bounds 2 px; wider bounds on grid/frame contacts, ragged strokes and merged symbols. The tiny lower circles were examined in separate enlarged crops. Triangle centers mean bounding-box centers. Upper drawn lines were followed separately with guides. The common helper reads actual dark-run centers at nominal 12 px (~2.06 thousandths) columns with small column shifts away from vertical gridlines; it never emits interpolated guide values. Bounds are half vertical run thickness. Overlays were checked. Actual gaps around symbols remain unsampled, and the audit lists skipped columns and >5-thousandths gaps. Lower panel has no drawn connecting lines. Features include first, peak, last and the visible shoulder/hump/terminal hook where applicable.

Legend: n4695 plus; n3815 circle; n2934 triangle-down; n1761 square; n734 triangle-up. All unit weights 58.7. The enlarged legend heading reads sigma (PSF), gamma (PCF). No numerical legend misprint was found.

Ambiguities and omissions:
- Upper n4695 has 11 visible pluses. No distinct plus is visible near 50 thousandths/u=1090; the curve passes through there. Its second plus near u=937 lies above the drawn curve. The end is a pronounced downward hook, not monotonic continuation.
- Upper n3815 circle near (969,2215) is well below the drawn rising line. Circle near u=1921 lies above the curve, and late circles lie slightly below the drawn plateau. These observed departures were not corrected to the curve.
- Upper triangle-down near (1092,2214) is on the vertical grid and substantially below its drawn curve. Square near u=1225 also departs from its drawn line. Several triangle-up markers sit above the low-stress trace.
- Upper n2934 has a shoulder then renewed rise, and n1761 has a local hump near 200 thousandths; these features are retained. Close extra marker spacing around u=1900-1984 is real and not regularized. Terminal markers overlap the right frame.
- Lower n4695: no separable plus at x=0 near the first triangle/circle cluster, nor near x=200 where the circle/triangle/grid share ink. These possible hidden markers are omitted and noted here. Only 12 identifiable pluses are recorded. A plus near (1855,3334) partially shares ink with the triangle and possible small circle; this cluster is assigned wide bounds.
- Lower n3815 circles are unusually small and look blocky in this scan. Many touch the bottoms of triangle-down symbols; others are close to pluses. The circle near (1850,3327) cannot be isolated fully from the common cluster, so its center has 10 px bounds and an explicit row note. A separate small circle at (2281,3329) is beyond the 250-thousandths frame and retained. The first circle at (795,3297) sits just left of the left frame. Native coordinates are not clamped.
- Grid/frame contacts in both panels have wider row bounds. Several lower squares have broken side/bottom strokes. The 100-thousandths vertical line is interrupted by a square; the 150-thousandths line by the plus band. The interruptions do not imply absent gridlines.
- The highest circle/triangle-up plateaus have no unique maximum x; features identify representative highest drawn segments, with bounds. The affine y-spacing limitation above is separate from pixel reading uncertainty.

The final point tables were regenerated with the common helper after its symbol-mask review; no interpolated point values were introduced.

Marker-center audit: final refinement also uses closed white interiors of hollow symbols and the two orthogonal bars of pluses when the outline joins line ink. Triangle interior-box centers receive a 1.5-px correction toward the vertex to recover outer symbol-box centers, with 5-px bounds when the outer outline is connected to other ink. If neither local method nor an isolated ink box succeeds, the by-eye center is retained with at least 6-px bounds and a row note. marker-center-audit.json records every seed, final center, method and bound. Final overlays were reviewed after refinement.

Final trace QA: symbol masks use two-dimensional extents, permitting readings on lines that pass distinctly above/below off-curve markers. All panel symbols are located before masks are applied. Candidate points that follow another guide branch more closely are excluded unless shared ink is explicitly flagged. Horizontal-grid ink is accepted only when the intended branch itself lies within 10 px of that grid. Search radius is 28 px; extra by-eye guide locations resolve curved initial limbs without expanding to unrelated ink. Only emitted ink samples, not guides, appear in points CSVs.

trace-gaps-over-5.csv enumerates each retained over-5-thousandths gap with its neighboring samples and nearby symbols. This documents where the requested regular coverage is interrupted, rather than supplying invented points.

The contact sheet unisolated-centers-review.png documents final targeted checks of attached/broken symbol outlines. Centers were corrected where the initial seed was offset from the visible symbol. Final marker-center-audit.json records the corrected seed positions. Trace masks were regenerated from these positions to prevent sampling symbol ink as a curve.

Final reproducibility: use the final reading-spec.json with out/read_helpers.py to regenerate tables and overlays. Temporary procedural editing scripts were removed; final specs and image crops are retained. Clean disconnected triangle outlines use the outer ink bounding box; connected outlines use the white-interior method with 5-px bounds.
