# FC-3C independent reading

Evidence used: packet/p0125.png and the packet protocol/metadata only. All pixel coordinates are on the unrotated original page. No other reading was sought or used.

Calibration: separate detect runs. Upper box (795,1070,2290,2850), bands 24, min-frac .55, snap 4, frame-frac .25. The first pass erroneously chose the soil-heading bottom border as y=6000 (RMS 79.190 psf) and was rejected. Lowering the coverage requirement exposed the true top frame, visible in 8/24 bands to the right of the heading. The rejected heading border is dropped in the final run. All final lines are detected, none predicted. Lower box (815,2968,2300,3580), bands 20, min-frac .55, snap 4, frame-frac .6; y=-100,0,+100. Upper RMS x .364 thousandths, y 9.666 psf; lower RMS x .312 thousandths, y .287 thousandths. These residuals are below .5% of the axis spans.

Eye checks: calibration-eye-checks.csv contains three labels per axis. Upper x at v=2700: 0,100,250. Upper y at u=2200: 6000,4000,0 (the 6000 check uses the visible right-hand frame). Lower x at v=3400: 0,100,250; lower y at u=900: +100,0,-100. Small grid-spacing irregularities and tilt are retained in the affine fit.

Markers were read first on labeled zoom-3 tiles. reading-spec.json preserves manual seeds. Clean isolated centers were refined to the midpoint of their local dark component bounding box; grid/overlap centers retain manual readings and wider bounds. Triangle centers are bounding-box centers. Trace guides are independent readings of line locations, not a fit to marker ordinates. The helper selects actual dark-run centers at nominal 12-px (~2.06 thousandths) columns, shifting slightly away from vertical gridlines. Additional by-eye guide locations handle the strongly curved initial rising limbs. Emitted y values come from ink, never guide interpolation. Bounds are half the vertical run thickness, widened for merged grid strokes. The overlays were inspected. Deliberate gaps around markers are preserved; over-5-thousandths gaps in the sampling audit fall at these interruptions or avoided marker/grid contacts. The lower panel has no drawn connecting lines, so it contains markers only. Each upper curve has first, peak and last features; broad peaks have uncertain x locations.

Legend: plus n4695, circle n3815, triangle-down n2934, square n1761, triangle-up n734; all densities 75.0. Normal-stress heading prints sigma. No legend numerical misprint was found here.

Ambiguities:
- Heading hides most of the upper 6000-psf frame; correct calibration is anchored by its visible right-hand stretch, not the heading border.
- Upper n1761: no distinct square around u=1550 (~130 thousandths), although the drawn line is continuous. This possible missing marker is omitted and noted. The other upper series each have 12 markers.
- Upper first n4695 plus is on the 4000-psf grid; first n734 triangle is on the 1000-psf grid. The n2934 marker near 200 thousandths touches the 3000-psf grid. Several rightmost symbols overlap the frame or a horizontal grid, with bounds recorded in their rows.
- Upper n734 descending trace briefly coincides with the 1000-psf grid, and the circle and square terminal traces approach the 4000/2000-psf grids. Shared ink is sampled with widened bounds; it is not claimed to be independently separable.
- Lower early symbols are crowded around x=0 and y=0. Circle at (829,3330), triangle-up at (829,3307), and plus at (829,3355) overlap the tilted frame; first square and triangle-down near u=860 are close. Separate early circle near u=891 is visible and retained. Circle series thus has 14 lower markers, not 13.
- Lower circle/plus near u=1410 and 1520, circle/triangle near u=1640 and 1700, and circle/triangle near u=2112-2137 partially share or nearly touch ink. Assignments follow visible symbol outlines. Bounds are 7-10 px where necessary; no common value is substituted.
- Lower frame markers at about u=2280 and grid contacts at about u=1120,1410,1700,1997 have wide bounds. Broken square/triangle strokes are retained where identity remains visible. Coordinates are not clamped to the native axis range.
- The lowest-stress and square crests are broad, so the peak feature x is a representative highest drawn segment, not a uniquely localized maximum. Some markers depart from the author-drawn line; traces follow the line.

Deliverables: cal-p1/anchors.csv, cal-p1/calibration.json, cal-p2/anchors.csv, cal-p2/calibration.json, calibration-eye-checks.csv, points-p1.csv, points-p1-native.csv, points-p2.csv, points-p2-native.csv, overlay-p1.png, overlay-p2.png, notes.md. Supporting counts.json, reading-spec.json, trace-sampling-audit.json, zoomed labeled tiles and helper scripts preserve the reading process; common helper out/read_helpers.py.

Final bounds review: peak feature horizontal bounds were widened to cover the visually plausible crest/plateau location. Vertical bounds remain tied to ink/center reading uncertainty. The final point tables were regenerated with the common helper after its symbol-mask review; no interpolated point values were introduced.

Marker-center audit: final refinement also uses closed white interiors of hollow symbols and the two orthogonal bars of pluses when the outline joins line ink. Triangle interior-box centers receive a 1.5-px correction toward the vertex to recover outer symbol-box centers, with 5-px bounds when the outer outline is connected to other ink. If neither local method nor an isolated ink box succeeds, the by-eye center is retained with at least 6-px bounds and a row note. marker-center-audit.json records every seed, final center, method and bound. Final overlays were reviewed after refinement.

Final trace QA: symbol masks use two-dimensional extents, permitting readings on lines that pass distinctly above/below off-curve markers. All panel symbols are located before masks are applied. Candidate points that follow another guide branch more closely are excluded unless shared ink is explicitly flagged. Horizontal-grid ink is accepted only when the intended branch itself lies within 10 px of that grid. Search radius is 28 px; extra by-eye guide locations resolve curved initial limbs without expanding to unrelated ink. Only emitted ink samples, not guides, appear in points CSVs.

trace-gaps-over-5.csv enumerates each retained over-5-thousandths gap with its neighboring samples and nearby symbols. This documents where the requested regular coverage is interrupted, rather than supplying invented points.

The contact sheet unisolated-centers-review.png documents final targeted checks of attached/broken symbol outlines. Centers were corrected where the initial seed was offset from the visible symbol. Final marker-center-audit.json records the corrected seed positions. Trace masks were regenerated from these positions to prevent sampling symbol ink as a curve.

Final reproducibility: use the final reading-spec.json with out/read_helpers.py to regenerate tables and overlays. Temporary procedural editing scripts were removed; final specs and image crops are retained. Clean disconnected triangle outlines use the outer ink bounding box; connected outlines use the white-interior method with 5-px bounds.
