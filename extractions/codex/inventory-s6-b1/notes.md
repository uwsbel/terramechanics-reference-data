## Text and figure discrepancies

- At 5-69 (PDF 349), the third, RSM-b group is described as having “the same initial conditions as that of the first group,” which is the 45-degree RS series. F6-8 gives Bendix 63 a yaw angle of 22.5 degrees and says of Bendix 68, “Soil not rolled.” F5-41 and F6-9 omit both drops. The inset of F5-41 also says “RS Dense Soil,” while the caption, Bendix IDs, F6-8 and the prose identify RSM-b.
- Bed preparation prose (5-68, PDF 348) says “layers about four inches thick, followed by rolling 20 times (10 back and forth cycles).” The first remark in F6-6 (6-9, PDF 367) says “10 rolls per two-inch layer.” The descriptions differ in layer thickness and rolling count.
- At 6-6 (PDF 364), the rigid-series prose says the vehicle “had a yaw angle of 22.5 degrees.” The damaged Bendix 49 cell in F6-4 (6-7, PDF 365) looks like 22.3. The surrounding rows give 22.5.
- At 6-6 (PDF 364), the report says of the RS dense series, “These results are plotted in Figure 6-7.” Bendix 25 has VH 5 in F6-6 (6-9, PDF 367), but plots at VH 6 in F6-7 (6-10, PDF 368) and F5-40 (5-70, PDF 350), with VV 12 in all three.
- The introduction to detail correlation (6-14, PDF 372) says it compares “computed and experimental footpad penetrations, velocities, velocity angles, and ground reactions.” The detail comparison sheets show motion and penetration, with no ground reaction panel. Section V says it was decided “not to include these footpad forces” (5-66, PDF 346) because strain-gage noise made them unreliable. It points instead to the strut axial-load examples in F5-25 and F5-32. Those are Bendix 10 and 11, or NASA 1 and 2.
- At 6-21 (PDF 379), the repeatability sentence reads “Drop No. 3 same as No. 1, No. 4 same as No. 2”. F6-10 gives the same nominal VV/VH pair to NASA 3 and 2 (5/7 ft/s), and the same pair to NASA 4 and 1 (10/0 ft/s). The starting velocities and plotted motions support the table's pairing. F6-10 also appears to print NASA 5's Dr as “3” without a visible decimal, where its intermediate companion NASA 6 has “0.3” and the T5-3 agreed entry lists intermediate Dr .3.
- The variable list prints “Z - vehicle c.g. vertical position - inch” and uses Z for its time derivatives (6-14 to sequence page 6-16, PDF 372 and 374). The plots label vertical position and derivatives X. The pitch rate and angle dot marks in the 6-14 prose list also differ from the pitch plot captions. The inventory uses the written quantities and the symbols in the plots.
- At 5-78 (PDF 358), “The slight geometric difference between the full-scale LM and the LM scale model (Figure 5-8)” points to the instrumented pad photo in the agreed index. F5-9 is the indexed configuration comparison.

Other computed axes have repeated or misplaced ticks, including the pitch-rate scale at PDF 381, the horizontal position scale at PDF 421, and the vertical-position time ticks at PDF 429.
