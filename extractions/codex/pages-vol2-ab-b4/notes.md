## Protocol and layout decisions

- Pages 75, 77, 84, 86, 92, and 95 require a quarter-turn clockwise; their headers use rotation 90. All other pages use rotation 0.
- The numbered specification headings are recorded as sections, using their printed single-part numbers. No Appendix A heading is printed in this batch, so A is not added to the section lists.
- Unnumbered tables use the supplied TA locator IDs, without invented body titles. The table continuing onto p0091 has no separate locator or repeated title, so that page's tables list is empty. Its entire table is nevertheless transcribed.
- The grain-size inset tables are transcribed in full outside the figure blocks; no new table IDs were invented. Multirow and merged table headers are represented with blank cells, keeping each printed label once. The classification strips are also represented as tables, with SAND covering the three printed sand divisions.
- `x2 U.S. Std. Sieve No.` records the printed upper horizontal scale label on the grain-size figures. Tick numbers, including sieve-number ticks, are omitted under rule 9. There is no separately printed curve legend on these pages; the figure blocks say `legend: none`.
- Printed density endpoint annotations outside the plot frames are retained in the body. No curve values were estimated or extracted. The photomicrograph scale captions are body text; the visible RSM label inside the p0092 photograph is also retained as body text because the fixed figure block has no annotation field.
- Paragraph continuation markers connect p0074 to p0076 and p0083 to p0085 across intervening figure pages. The paragraph beginning p0093 starts after the subsection label on p0091, rather than continuing a sentence, so it has no continuation marker.
- Underlining, rules, border damage, isolated marginal strokes, and bleed-through were omitted. Small marks that appeared to be printed punctuation were retained, as noted above.
