## Protocol and layout decisions

- Rotation is 90 for p0028, p0030, p0036, p0039, p0045, and p0047: a quarter turn clockwise makes the figure text upright. Other pages use 0.
- Numbered specification items are represented as level-one headings. Their printed numbers are included in `sections`; the unnumbered specification titles do not invent an appendix section heading.
- The grain-size inset tables have no separately assigned table IDs in the supplied index. They are fully transcribed as Markdown tables in the body, with the agreed figure ID in the header. Their two-tier headers and the classification bands use blank cells to represent merged areas without duplicating printed text.
- Photomicrograph scale annotations are body text. The visible RSM label in p0036 is also preserved as body text.
- Density figures have explicitly printed endpoint annotations outside the frames. These are body text; no values were estimated from curves. Numeric axis ticks, including the upper sieve-axis ticks, are omitted under rule 9. No figure in this batch has a separately printed series legend.
- The paragraph ending on p0027 resumes on p0029 after an intervening photomicrograph. Continuation markers connect those text pages. The item label on p0034 continues with its table on p0035, but is not a split prose paragraph.
