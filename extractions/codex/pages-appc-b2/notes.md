# Transcription notes: pages-appc-b2

All 22 supplied page images, PDF 138 through 159, were read directly and the page transcriptions checked against them. Enlarged crops and review plates are retained in this directory. No OCR, text-recognition tool, network source, or separate transcription was used.

## Hesitations and decisions

- PDF 138, footer: the last digit of C-24 is partly lost. Read as C-24 from the surviving strokes; this agrees with the supplied inferred label. The header records C-24.
- PDFs 138-151, fit annotations: several equals signs are heavily inked and resemble solid rectangles. Enlarged examples show the two horizontal strokes, so they are transcribed as `=`. Thin horizontal streaks continuing from letters, numbers, or captions were treated as scan artifacts, not additional hyphens or punctuation.
- PDF 147, cohesion annotation: transcribed `Cohesion = -1.87 [?]`. The sign before 1.87 has extra vertical and lower ink that can resemble an overprinted character or a plus/minus sign. I chose a minus sign from the horizontal remnant, but the sign remains uncertain after enlargement. This is a reading of the printed annotation, not a value calculated from the plotted line.
- PDF 149, cohesion annotation: transcribed `584.29`. The top of the first digit is broken; the surviving upper and lower strokes support 5. Enlarged crop checked.
- PDF 151, cohesion annotation: transcribed `213.76 [?]`. The first digit after the decimal is damaged. Its surviving upper stroke and descending remnant were read as 7; this remains uncertain after enlargement.
- PDFs 152-159, vertical axis: the symbol is transcribed as `$φ_{DS}$`, with both D and S in the subscript. The label is `$φ_{DS}$ - Degrees`; the horizontal axis is `$D_r$`. On PDF 158 the separating dash is faint but visible in the rotated axis crop.
- PDF 159, curve labels: read `RC2`, `PS`, `RS`, `RSM-b`, `RSM-a`, and `SS`, in top-to-bottom label order. The hyphens in RSM-b and RSM-a belong to the soil names.

The only `[?]` markers in the page files are on PDF 147 and PDF 151, documented above. No `[illegible]` or `[sic]` markers were needed.

## Application of the protocol

- This batch contains failure-envelope figures and friction-angle figures, with no data-sheet legend tables, prose page, numbered table, or numbered equation.
- The soil headings and the four printed fit annotations on PDF 138-151 are body text before the figure block. Their numerical annotations are transcribed as printed; no marker coordinates, curve values, axis tick values, or tick ranges have been extracted.
- The soil labels on PDF 152-158 are likewise body text. They are printed within the graph frame but serve as figure headings; the revised block layout has no heading field.
- PDF 138-158 have no printed legend key. Their `legend:` field is left empty rather than inventing labels for the marker or line styles. Those visual features are described only in the one-sentence description.
- PDF 159 labels its curves directly rather than using a separate legend box. Those six printed labels are recorded as the legend entries, with no invented symbol names or added line-style descriptions in that field.
- The axis multipliers on PDF 138-151 are represented as `(x 10^1)`, preserving the printed x and superscript exponent in text notation.
- Underlines, isolated marks, edge damage, and horizontal scan streaks are omitted under rule 10.
