# Appendix A grain-size curves against their printed parameter tables

Source: AM-68-1 Vol. II (NASA CR-92215), Appendix A, Figs. A-2, A-6, A-18, A-22, A-26, A-38, A-46
(printed A-6, A-13, A-38, A-46, A-53, A-76, A-94; PDF 7, 14, 39, 47, 54, 77, 95). Each chart draws an
average grain-size curve and the range of several samples (two dashed curves) on log paper, and prints a
table of grain-size parameters inside the frame. Both agents read the curves blind (batch
`curves-appa-b2`, 2026-10-15, the log axis of D-027); the values below are interpolated in log grain
size along the reconciled average curve. Findings, 2026-10-15:

1. **The tables and the drawn curves agree to a few percent**, the drawn D50 a little finer than printed
   on A-2 and A-6 (3 to 6 percent) and coarser on A-26 (4 percent), D95 within 5 percent except A-22
   (5.27 drawn against 5.0 printed). Near D50 the curves are steep, so the reading bound in grain size is
   about 1 percent; the differences are the drafting of the curves against numbers computed from the
   sieve data, not reading errors. The tables are the authors' numbers; the curves are kept as read.
2. **A-26's printed Cu is 1.5, which is its printed D50 over D10 (0.42 / 0.28), while the drawn curve
   gives D60 / D10 = 1.67.** A uniformity coefficient is D60 / D10. On the other charts the printed Cu
   agrees with the drawn D60 / D10 within 0.05, so this one value looks like a slip in the table; one
   chart is not enough to say more.
3. **Printed circles on the curves are few.** Codex's reading recorded 168 circles at the standard
   sieve sizes; in blind adjudication both adjudicators confirmed 7 (three on A-2, three on A-22, one on A-6), rejected
   or could not confirm the rest (the curves run together on 1-bit scans, so a filled ring cannot be
   told from merged strokes). The unconfirmed ones stay in the records as one-sided points and should
   not be used as sieve data.
