# Appendix C envelopes checked against their own data sheets

Source: AM-68-1 Vol. II (NASA CR-92215), Appendix C. Every failure envelope (Figs. C-1A to C-7A)
plots the peak shear stresses of the direct shear tests on the data sheets that follow it. With both
read independently and reconciled (batches `curves-appc-b1` to `-b6`), each envelope point can be
compared with the peak of its own test. Findings, 2026-10-14:

1. **Fig. C-2A (PS soil, printed C-6, PDF 120) has its legend swapped.** As printed, the squares are
   the loose group (Dr .33 to .53) on the steeper 49.5-degree line and the circles the dense group
   (Dr 1.15 to 1.3) on the 46.7-degree line, which would make loose PS stronger than dense. The
   squares are within 8 to 48 psf of the peaks of the dense tests (Fig. C-2C, printed C-8, PDF 122)
   and 150 to 770 psf from the loose ones (Fig. C-2B, printed C-7, PDF 121); the circles are within 4
   to 54 psf of the loose peaks. Fig. C-17 (printed C-39, PDF 153) plots 46.7 degrees at the loose Dr.
   **Loose PS: 46.7 degrees; dense PS: 49.5 degrees.** The records `b68-ds-fc-2a-dr33-53` and
   `b68-ds-fc-2a-dr115-130` carry the corrected pairing; the printed legend text is kept as printed.
2. **Fig. C-4A (RC2, printed C-12, PDF 126)**: two points of the dense group sit 150 to 200 psf below
   the peaks of the corresponding tests of Fig. C-4C (printed C-14, PDF 128), in both readings; a
   difference within the report, not a reading error. The other envelope points sit 10 to 46 psf
   above the sheets' peaks (the envelopes are drafted on tick-only axes; part of the offset is the
   drafting of their scales).
3. **Fig. C-1A (RS, printed C-2, PDF 116)**: three symbols sit on another group's line (a filled
   circle at 315 psf on the 45.5-degree dashed extension, the triangle at zero normal stress, a
   filled circle at 1772 psf on the 51-degree line); their peaks identify them as tests of Figs. C-1C
   and C-1D, so the drawn lines pass through symbols of other groups near the axis. The triangle at
   zero normal stress is the zero-stress test of C-1D (476 psf), which Fig. C-9A's printed fit
   (tan 1.3059, cohesion 634.20 psf) also includes.
4. **Fig. C-6A (LSM, printed C-17, PDF 131) prints no friction angles and no line labels.** Its
   groups are identified by the data sheets (batch `curves-appc-b6`, both readings): the circles
   are the loose tests of Fig. C-6B (printed C-18, PDF 132), within 4 to 35 psf of their peaks; the
   squares the dense tests of Fig. C-6C (printed C-19, PDF 133), within 5 to 51 psf; the triangles
   the tests of Fig. C-6D (printed C-20, PDF 134), 24 to 90 psf below their peaks. A mark one reader
   took for an unlisted downward triangle at 2946 psf is the circle of the 2934 psf test of C-6B
   (18 psf from its peak). The dense group's line is the flattest of the three; that is in the data
   sheets themselves (dense peaks 3019 psf at 3815 psf and 4018 at 4695, against 3756 and 4252 for
   the loose group), not a reading error.
5. **Fig. C-6C prints its second test as 1961 psf; Fig. C-6A plots it at about 1740 psf.** The
   envelope's square sits at a normal stress of 1739 to 1742 psf (both readings) with the C-6C
   peak to 1 psf (1398 against 1399). The other LSM sheets use 1761 psf (and C-6C its own 714 psf
   for the lightest test). Either the legend's 1961 or the envelope's position is a drafting slip;
   the pages do not say which. The record keeps 1961 as printed.
6. **Fig. C-5A (SS, printed C-15, PDF 129)**: only the first group (circles, Dr .02 to .13) has data
   sheets here (Fig. C-5B, printed C-16, PDF 130). Three circles are within 25 psf of their tests'
   peaks; the 734 psf one sits 55 psf above it and the 3815 psf one 104 psf below it, in both
   readings. The other three groups come from Bendix Report No. MM-65-2 (as printed on the figure;
   the figures index read MM-66-2) and cannot be checked here.
7. **Fig. C-7A (RSM-b, printed C-21, PDF 135)**: every symbol is within 0.4 psi of the peak of its
   test (dotted circles: Fig. C-7C, Dr 0.53; dotted triangles: Fig. C-7B, Dr 1.0).

Use: when quoting a friction angle or cohesion of these soils from Appendix C, take the group from
the data sheets, not from the printed legend of C-2A. Cite as `AM-68-1 Vol. II, Fig. C-2A, printed
C-6, PDF 120` with this note.
