Recovered observations

57 points (from F2-29) read by two readings made separately and reconciled (batch curves-s2-b1, rules docs/CURVE-READING.md (2026-10-08)). Values are SI; the printed-unit values and the pixel coordinates on the page image are kept beside them so every point can be checked against the scan. x_read_bound and y_read_bound are reading bounds in printed units: the largest change of value over the point’s pixel interval on the piecewise map, plus the location error of the two lines around it (D-025; the global calibration residual is not added, see qa//calibration-sensitivity.md for the affine difference); x_bound_si and y_bound_si are the same in SI. They are not standard deviations.
