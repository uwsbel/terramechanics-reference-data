## FC-1B

Figure C-1B: Direct Shear Test Data - Shear Stress vs. Shear Displacement; Dilation Displacement vs. Shear Displacement (printed C-3, PDF 117)

Rotation 0 degrees. Legend: legend at the right of the figure. Tests and symbols: 1172 psf (56.1 kPa): triangle-down; 1760 psf (84.3 kPa): plus; 2345 psf (112.3 kPa): square; 2935 psf (140.5 kPa): asterisk; 294 psf (14.1 kPa): circle; 3520 psf (168.5 kPa): x; 587 psf (28.1 kPa): triangle-up.

Seven direct shear tests on RS soil (red volcanic cinder) at Dr .50 to .57, normal stresses 294 to 3520 psf. Upper panel: shear stress 0 to 6000 psf against shear displacement 0 to 250 thousandths of an inch, a hand-drawn curve through each test's symbols. Lower panel: dilation -100 to +100 thousandths, symbols only, same notation as above. Hard spots: the small asterisks (2935 psf) look like blobs; near the origin the circles and down-triangles touch; symbols on the frame or on grid lines are partly hidden; the 1760 and 2345 psf curves share one line of ink from about 35 to 48 thousandths and then fork; a stray straight ink line runs under the 2345 psf curve between 170 and 200 thousandths; the scan is tilted by about one degree. The same figure is printed as Figure 2-21 in Volume I.
