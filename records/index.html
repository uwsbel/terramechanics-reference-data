Project records

How the collection is built and checked, kept public because the project is open and invites help. None of this is needed to use the data.

  1. Decisions: the decisions that shaped the method, with their options.
  2. Review: the record of human checks, and every value that rests on one reading only.
  3. Open questions: what could not be settled, with the evidence, and how to help.
  4. Methods: data organization, the reading rules and how the agents work.
  5. Insights: judgment and caveats the report authors recorded.
  6. Figures and Sources: every figure of the report sections indexed, and the register of sources.

Five counts that measure different things

count value what it counts
Indexed records 348 rows in the experiment index: single tests, averaged and derived series, correlations, text summaries and bin samples
Single physical tests 142 rows of kind single: one physical test on one bed
Tests with complete printed setup 4 rows whose bed state, apparatus geometry, bin and loading rate are all printed in the source
Observation series extracted 192 experiments with an observations.csv (digitized points) in data/experiments/
Human audits recorded 3360 rows in qa/audits.csv; agreement between the two agents is not an audit
