Load-penetration test (LPT) 24: 4-inch (102 mm) cylinder pushed into dense red scoria (RS)

LPT No. 24 in the report; record b68-lpt-024

A cylinder 101.6 mm (4 in) in diameter was pushed into a bed of dense red scoria (RS), and the load on it was recorded against its penetration. This is a load-penetration test (LPT), run at 0.423 mm/s in a bed of bulk density 825 kg/m³.

Data available: 26 points read from the printed figure. One of them is still under review. Student reviewers have checked 7 of them on the scan, and two reviewers confirmed 6.

Codes on this card: RS, red crushed volcanic scoria, narrowly graded; Gp, peak deceleration of the dynamic penetrometer, in g; D50, grain size that half of the soil, by weight, is finer than; D95, grain size that 95 percent of the soil, by weight, is finer than; Cu, uniformity coefficient.

A simulation of this test is compared with the measurement through the axial force the soil exerts on the cylinder, the force the load cell measured, against the cylinder’s penetration below the original soil surface. A comparison states where it puts the zero of force and of penetration, and whether the cylinder’s own weight is included. The Simulate a test page walks through the steps.

The CSV file gives penetration in m (x_si) and load in N (y_si), each with its reading bound (x_bound_si, y_bound_si), and the status of each point. The JSON file gives the units, the conversion factors and how the points were read. A reading bound says how precisely a point could be read from the printed figure, typically 0.55 mm in penetration and 21 N in load here. It is not the scatter of the experiment, which the report does not give. Both readings drawn over the scanned figure.
