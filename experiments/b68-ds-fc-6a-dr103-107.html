Direct shear of LSM soil, gamma 91 to 93.3 Lb./Ft.^3 (Dr = 1.03 to 1.07)

Direct shear test on LSM soil at relative density 1.03 to 1.07; apparatus: direct shear (University of Notre Dame). Recorded: peak shear stress vs normal stress.

The numbers below were digitized from the published figure by two readings made separately; they agree within the stated reading bounds at 24 of the 26 points, and 1 is still under review. Student reviewers have looked at 8 of the 26 points on the scan; none has yet been confirmed by two reviewers (see the provenance page). The test setup is incomplete: specimen height, normal stress, held constant, displacement rate are not reported.

Data

Peak maximum shear stress 194.2 kPa at 218.9 kPa normal stress, the end of the record.

Download: observations.csv (26 points: 5 plotted symbols, 20 samples of the drawn curve; columns x_si, y_si with bounds x_bound_si, y_bound_si; panel 1: maximum shear stress in Pa against normal stress in Pa; the printed-unit values, the pixel position on the scan and the review status of each point are kept in further columns) and series.json (units, conversion factors, how the points were read).

Reading bounds on this figure (how far our reading of the printed figure may be off, not the uncertainty of the test): typical bound 183 Pa in normal stress, 1.3 kPa in maximum shear stress (0.7 percent of the peak). Both independent readings agree within these bounds at 24 of the 26 points; 1 is still under review (hollow in the plot) and 1 was settled against the scan. Check the digitized points against the scan.
