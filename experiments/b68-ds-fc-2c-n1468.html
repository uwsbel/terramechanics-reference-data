Direct shear of PS soil at 70.3 kPa normal stress

Direct shear test on PS soil at relative density 1.15 to 1.3; apparatus: direct shear (University of Notre Dame); loading: normal stress 1468 psf. Recorded: shear stress and dilation vs shear displacement. One of 6 tests of this soil and density at normal stresses from 35.1 to 210.7 kPa, the family the report used to draw the failure envelope.

The numbers below were digitized from the published figure by two readings made separately; they agree within the stated reading bounds at 21 of the 34 points, and 10 are still under review. Student reviewers have looked at 12 of the 34 points on the scan; two reviewers confirmed 2 of them (see the provenance page). The test setup is incomplete: specimen height, displacement rate are not reported.

Data

Peak shear stress 97.5 kPa at 1.99 mm shear displacement, the end of the record. Dilation 442 micrometers at the end.

Download: observations.csv (34 points: 13 plotted symbols, 19 samples of the drawn curve; columns x_si, y_si with bounds x_bound_si, y_bound_si; panel 1: shear stress in Pa against shear displacement in m; panel 2: dilation (normal displacement; positive up) in m against shear displacement in m; the printed-unit values, the pixel position on the scan and the review status of each point are kept in further columns) and series.json (units, conversion factors, how the points were read).

Reading bounds on this figure (how far our reading of the printed figure may be off, not the uncertainty of the test): typical bound 11 micrometers in shear displacement, 744 Pa in shear stress (0.8 percent of the peak), 23 micrometers in dilation. Both independent readings agree within these bounds at 21 of the 34 points; 10 are still under review (hollow in the plot) and 3 were settled against the scan. Check the digitized points against the scan.
